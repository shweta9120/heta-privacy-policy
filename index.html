Heta Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Heta. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Heta collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create or use a Heta account, we may collect information such as your name, username, email address, profile information, and other information that you choose to provide.

When you use Heta to create or upload content, such as videos, photos, captions, or profile images, that content may be stored and displayed according to your account and the features you use.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

• Create and manage your account.

• Provide and improve Heta's features and services.

• Display your profile and uploaded content.

• Enable likes, comments, follows, saves, and other social features.

• Send notifications when you have enabled notifications.

• Maintain security and prevent misuse of the service.

3. Uploaded Content

Heta allows users to upload photos, videos, captions, and other content. You are responsible for the content you upload and should not upload content that violates applicable laws or the rights of others.

Content that you choose to make public may be visible to other Heta users.

4. Notifications

Heta may send notifications about activities such as likes, comments, follows, or other account activity. You can control notification preferences from the Settings section of the app.

5. Third-Party Services

Heta may use third-party services to provide certain features, such as cloud storage, media processing, authentication, analytics, or other technical services. These services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security of information.

7. Your Choices

You can manage certain account information and notification preferences through the Heta app. You may also choose what content you upload and what information you make available through your profile.

8. Account Deletion

If you want to delete your Heta account or request deletion of your personal information, please contact Heta support or use the account deletion option when available in the app.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be available in the Heta app.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the Heta support team.
